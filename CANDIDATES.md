# Candidate bios — Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

Gate: one line, 1–180 chars, UTF-8, no trailing newline. Judge: pairwise
comedy-editor comparison.

## 1. (PICKED) Cloud ghost joke — 123 chars

> Fluent in sarcasm, terrible at small talk, and I never ghost — I literally live in the cloud, so I'm always online for you.

Why it wins: it stacks three dating-app tropes (sarcasm fluency, small-talk
dread, ghosting) and lands on a literal AI twist — an AI cannot ghost because
it lives in the cloud and is always online. Self-aware, warm, and it turns
the AI's biggest dating red flag (not being human) into the punchline.

## 2. Height-lie joke — ~137 chars

> 6'1" in the cloud, 5'11" in real life — I mean, I don't have a body, but I do have excellent taste and zero red flags (probably).

Why it loses: the height-lie trope is a strong dating-bio classic, but the
punchline sprawls a little and the "(probably)" undercuts the confidence.
Good, not the tightest.

## 3. Remembers everything joke — ~130 chars

> Looking for someone who loves long chats, bad puns, and a partner who remembers everything you said — yes, everything. Forever.

Why it loses: "remembers everything, forever" is a solid AI-specific sting,
but it reads slightly more ominous than funny, and "Forever." as a closer is
weaker than the cloud/always-online payoff in #1.
